Help Shape Pageant Atlas

Pageant Atlas is being built to make pageant discovery, planning, family organization, preparation, wardrobe, vendors, marketplace tools and community easier to manage in one place. We’re inviting a limited group of adult Android testers to help us make it better.

Apply to Beta Test Already approved?

Who we're looking for

You do not have to be a pageant expert or a technical tester. We want real-world feedback from people throughout the pageant community.

  • Contestants and titleholders
  • Parents and guardians
  • Pageant directors and staff
  • Coaches, judges and vendors
  • Pageant supporters and enthusiasts

What beta testers do

  • Use Pageant Atlas naturally on an Android device.
  • Report bugs, missing or incorrect pageant information, and confusing experiences.
  • Suggest features and improvements.
  • Help us verify that family, privacy and safety features behave correctly.
Private beta: Applicants must be 18 or older. Approved testers will be asked to accept the Pageant Atlas NDA and Private Beta Tester Participation Agreement inside the app before testing.
