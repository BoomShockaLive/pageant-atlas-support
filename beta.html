Accuracy matters

Missing or incorrect pageant information is valuable beta feedback. When official information cannot be verified, the product should say so rather than inventing details.

Frequently asked questions

Before you apply.

Does it cost anything to beta test Pageant Atlas?

No. Participation in the Pageant Atlas private beta is free.

Do I need pageant experience?

No. Pageant-community experience is especially useful, but we also need feedback about general usability, navigation, accessibility and reliability.

What devices are supported?

The current closed beta is focused on Android devices distributed through Google Play.

Can my child use Pageant Atlas?

Pageant Atlas includes family and age-related safeguards. The current private beta application process is limited to eligible adult testers, while parent-managed and family experiences are part of the product being tested.

What if I find incorrect pageant information?

Please report it. Accurate pageant information is one of the most important goals of Pageant Atlas, and testers help us identify missing or incorrect records.

Where do I report problems?

Use the Beta Feedback tools inside Pageant Atlas whenever possible. If the app itself prevents you from reporting a problem, contact bigboomcreations@gmail.com.
